---
title: "Retrieval lanes"
description: "What depth:0, depth:1 and depth:2 select, and why the methodology does not iterate"
---

`depth:` selects which of three lanes a recall takes through the graph, trading precision against recall.

| Lane | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `depth:0` | the default — stops at the seed mass, which *is* the BM25 floor, and skips the anchor traversal entirely: the fast lane, on the text and vector indexes alone |
| `depth:1` | runs the anchor-mediated round, but admits an anchor only once its observed mass clears **twice** its null-expected share. Only strongly above-chance anchors speak, so what transmission adds is high-precision |
| `depth:2` | runs the same round admitting at the plain fair share — every anchor holding any surplus at all transmits, for maximum recall |

Only the **admission bar** differs between 1 and 2; the traversal, the null model and the attenuation are identical.

```text
recall billing entity:acme depth:1
```

## Why 2 is the ceiling

A larger depth is rejected rather than silently answered as 2. The methodology does not iterate: a second round re-observes the first round's own concentrated mass through sibling anchors and collapses recall, so iterated transmission stays reserved for a future generalization.

`max-depth` is the operator-set ceiling (2), and `default-depth` is the lane a recall takes when it omits the clause (0). See [Database settings](/docs/configuration/reference/database).

## Choosing a lane

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| the cheapest, most literal answer | `depth:0` |
| graph evidence, but only where it is strongly above chance | `depth:1` |
| everything the graph can justify | `depth:2` |

<Note title="Never below the BM25 floor" type="note">

Because transmission only ever adds, raising the lane cannot push a result below where plain text search would have put it — see [BM25 floor](/docs/retrieval/ranking/guarantees).

</Note>

## Lanes open from the seeds

A recall enters the graph through the anchors its seeds are filed under: every topic and entity the top text and vector matches are about is observed, whether or not the recall names it. An agent asking a question rarely knows how the answer was filed, and a lane that only acted through a name it had to guess would never engage — so the door is the matches themselves. Admission against the background rate is what bounds the expansion: a hub the matches merely share holds its fair share and stays silent, named or not, so opening from every seed costs nothing the method does not already price.

A named `topic:` or `entity:` is a filter on what the lane returns, not what lets it run: the round observes every anchor the seeds touch, then only facts filed under a named anchor are kept.

| Query | What runs |
|---|---|
| `recall billing depth:2` | the `depth:2` lane, through whatever the `billing` matches are filed under |
| `recall billing entity:acme depth:2` | the same round, keeping only facts filed under `acme` |

A recall seeded by anchors alone — no term and no vector — takes its candidates straight from the facts filed under those anchors, so there is no further step for a lane to add.
