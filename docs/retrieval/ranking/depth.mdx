---
title: "Retrieval lanes"
description: "What depth:0, depth:1 and depth:2 select, and why the methodology does not iterate"
---

`depth:` selects which of three lanes a recall takes through the graph, trading precision against recall.

| Lane | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `depth:0` | the default — skips the anchor round entirely: the fast lane, on the text and vector indexes and the anchors the recall names |
| `depth:1` | runs the anchor-mediated round, but admits an anchor only once its observed mass clears **twice** its null-expected share. Only strongly above-chance anchors speak, so what transmission adds is high-precision |
| `depth:2` | runs the same round admitting at the plain fair share — every anchor holding any surplus at all transmits, for maximum recall |

Only the **admission bar** differs between 1 and 2; the traversal, the null model and the fold are identical.

```text
recall billing entity:acme depth:1
```

## Why 2 is the ceiling

A larger depth is rejected rather than silently answered as 2. The methodology does not iterate: a second round would re-observe the first round's own concentrated mass through sibling anchors and collapse recall.

`max-depth` is the operator-set ceiling (2), and `default-depth` is the lane a recall takes when it omits the clause (0). See [Database settings](/docs/configuration/reference/database).

## Choosing a lane

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| the cheapest, most literal answer | `depth:0` |
| graph evidence, but only where it is strongly above chance | `depth:1` |
| everything the graph can justify | `depth:2` |

<Note title="The BM25 floor depends on the fold" type="note">

Under the `excess` fold transmission only ever adds, so raising the lane never lowers a result's score below what text and vector search alone give it. The `information` fold, the default, weighs the graph against the text, so raising the lane can reorder what the words found. See [BM25 floor](/docs/retrieval/ranking/guarantees#bm25-floor).

</Note>

## Lanes act through anchors

A recall enters the graph through the `topic:` and `entity:` anchors it names. A named anchor raises the facts filed under it in every lane, `depth:0` included (see [Named anchors](/docs/retrieval/ranking/hybrid-retrieval#named-anchors)); the lane decides whether the round also runs, through the other anchors the recall's matches are filed under. A recall that names none has nothing for a lane to act through: it runs on the text and vector indexes alone, exactly as `depth:0` would, and a `depth:1` or `depth:2` on it comes back with a [warning](/docs/query-language/reference/warnings) saying so.

| Query | What runs |
|---|---|
| `recall billing depth:2` | text and vector search only, with a warning |
| `recall billing entity:acme depth:0` | text and vector search, with `acme` raising the facts filed under it |
| `recall billing entity:acme depth:2` | the same, and the `depth:2` round through the other anchors the matches are filed under |

A recall seeded by anchors alone — no term and no vector — takes its candidates straight from the facts filed under those anchors, so there is no further step for a lane to add.
