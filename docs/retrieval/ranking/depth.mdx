---
title: "Retrieval lanes"
description: "What depth:0, depth:1 and depth:2 select, and why the methodology does not iterate"
---

`depth:` selects which of three lanes a recall takes through the graph, trading precision against recall.

| Lane | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `depth:0` | the default — skips the anchor traversal entirely: the fast lane, on the text and vector indexes alone |
| `depth:1` | runs the anchor-mediated round, but admits an anchor only once its observed mass clears **twice** its null-expected share. Only strongly above-chance anchors speak, so what transmission adds is high-precision |
| `depth:2` | runs the same round admitting at the plain fair share — every anchor holding any surplus at all transmits, for maximum recall |

Only the **admission bar** differs between 1 and 2; the traversal and the null model are identical. The bar applies to the anchors the seeds discover: an anchor the recall names clears either.

```text
recall billing entity:acme depth:1
```

## Why 2 is the ceiling

A larger depth is rejected rather than silently answered as 2. The methodology does not iterate: a second round would re-observe the first round's own concentrated mass through sibling anchors and collapse recall.

`max-depth` is the operator-set ceiling (2), and `default-depth` is the lane a recall takes when it omits the clause (0). See [Database settings](/docs/configuration/reference/database).

## Choosing a lane

| You want | Use |
|---|---|
| the cheapest, most literal answer | `depth:0` |
| graph evidence, but only where it is strongly above chance | `depth:1` |
| everything the graph can justify | `depth:2` |

<Note title="The BM25 floor depends on the fold" type="note">

Under the `excess` fold transmission only ever adds, so raising the lane never lowers a result's score below what text and vector search alone give it. Under the `information` fold, the default, each channel's $z^+$ is never negative, but the graph's facts join the pool the text is measured against, so raising the lane can lower a match's score and reorder what the words found. See [BM25 floor](/docs/retrieval/ranking/guarantees#bm25-floor).

</Note>

## Lanes act through anchors

A recall enters the graph through the `topic:` and `entity:` anchors it names. A recall that names none has nothing for a lane to act through: it runs on the text and vector indexes alone, exactly as `depth:0` would, and a `depth:1` or `depth:2` on it comes back with a [warning](/docs/query-language/reference/warnings) saying so.

An anchor beside a term or vector takes part only through what it transmits, and `depth:0` transmits nothing. So such a recall runs at `depth:1` at least: an omitted depth below that is raised in silence, and a written `depth:0` is raised with a warning.

| Query | What runs |
|---|---|
| `recall billing depth:2` | text and vector search only, with a warning |
| `recall billing entity:acme` | the `depth:1` lane, through `acme` |
| `recall billing entity:acme depth:0` | the `depth:1` lane, with a warning |
| `recall billing entity:acme depth:2` | the `depth:2` lane, through `acme` |

A recall seeded by anchors alone — no term and no vector — takes its candidates straight from the facts filed under those anchors, so there is no further step for a lane to add.

## An explained recall

An [explained recall](/docs/http-api/endpoints/explain) shows a lane at work: the anchors the seeds reached, the bar each had to clear, and the facts that came in on what the anchors transmitted. Sixteen facts under four anchors, on a server with the [window](/docs/configuration/reference/database#window) off (`window-gamma = -1`), so every text sighting is a fact's own match:

```text
remember 'acme sent the march invoice' entity:acme topic:billing
remember 'acme renewed its support contract' entity:acme
remember 'the april invoice is overdue' topic:billing
remember 'the may invoice was paid by card' topic:billing
remember 'billing closes on the last friday of the month' topic:billing
remember 'the supplier invoice arrived by post' topic:suppliers
remember 'the supplier list is kept on the shared drive' topic:suppliers
remember 'scan every invoice on the office printer' topic:office
remember 'the office opens at eight' topic:office
remember 'the office kitchen has a new kettle' topic:office
remember 'office plants are watered on mondays' topic:office
remember 'the office wifi password changed' topic:office
remember 'visitors sign in at the office desk' topic:office
remember 'the office closes for the holidays' topic:office
remember 'the office fire drill is on thursday' topic:office
remember 'the office lift is out of order' topic:office
```

The recall names `acme` beside a term and writes its lane:

```text
explain recall invoice entity:acme depth:1
```

The text index read one term, `invoic`, the stem of `invoice`, and the five facts that hold it are the `text` seeds. Nothing else seeded: no vector was bound, named anchors seed only a recall with no term or vector, and the graph admits facts rather than seeding them.

The seeds reached four anchors, each observing the mass of the seeds filed under it: `billing` holds three invoices, $1.1202 + 1.2492 + 1.1202 = 3.4896$, and `acme`, `suppliers` and `office` one each. So $\rho_0 = (1.1202 + 3.4896 + 1.1202 + 1.0154)/(2 + 4 + 2 + 9) = 0.3968$, the `background`, and `admission` holds the two bars per unit of degree, $2\rho_0 = 0.7936$ for `depth:1` and $\rho_0 = 0.3968$ for `depth:2`:

| Anchor | Degree | Mass | Bar at `depth:1` | Bar at `depth:2` | At `depth:1` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `acme` | 2 | 1.1202 | none: named | none: named | transmits, since the recall names it |
| `billing` | 4 | 3.4896 | 3.1743 | 1.5872 | transmits |
| `suppliers` | 2 | 1.1202 | 1.5872 | 0.7936 | silent: over its fair share, short of twice it |
| `office` | 9 | 1.0154 | 7.1422 | 3.5711 | silent: one match among nine facts |

`billing` clears the bar and transmits to every fact filed under it. `billing closes on the last friday of the month` holds none of the words and comes back with `seed: false` and a single `graph` sighting through `billing`, unnamed: it was admitted by transmission. The bar decides only whether an anchor speaks; what it passes on is its surplus over the fair share, shared across its edges, so that fact receives $(3.4896 - 4 \times 0.3968)/4 = 0.4756$ of graph evidence. Beside it, `the april invoice is overdue` is a seed, and its own `text` sighting is part of the mass it receives a share of, so it gets $(3.4896 - 1.2492 - 4 \times 0.3968)/4 = 0.1633$: a fact never funds its own boost.

`acme` speaks because the recall named it. `acme renewed its support contract` comes back on it alone, its `graph` sighting marked `named`, with $1.1202/2 = 0.5601$ and no fair share to clear. The march invoice is the seed that funded `acme`, so `acme` adds it nothing, though the sighting is listed.

`suppliers` and `office` stay silent, and nothing reaches their other members: neither the supplier list nor any of the eight other office facts comes back, and the two matches filed there rank on their text alone. `admission` holds the next lane's bar as well: `suppliers` holds $1.1202$ against a fair share of $0.7936$, so the same recall at `depth:2` lets it transmit and brings back `the supplier list is kept on the shared drive`, while `office`, under even its fair share, stays silent in both lanes.

How the graph evidence enters each score is the fold's: [Explained recall](/docs/http-api/endpoints/explain) defines every field of the envelope and reads the fold over an example.
