---
title: "Sizing"
description: "Memory is the constraint — what a vector costs, what a graph costs, and what the plan cache counts"
---

- **Memory** is the constraint. Facts, their index entries, and their vectors all live in the heap; a vector costs `dimension × 4` bytes at `float32` (`× 8` at `float64`) plus its entry in every RP-tree.
- **`num-graphs`** costs almost nothing empty. Allocate for the isolation you want, not the load you have.
- **`cache-capacity`** is a count of query plans, not bytes. The default 1000 is generous for agents that repeat a handful of query shapes.

## What grows, and what does not

| Grows with | Bounded by |
|---|---|
| facts, entities, relationships | nothing — there is no eviction |
| vector forest entries | `flush-factor × live vectors`, enforced by rebuilds |
| the plan cache | `cache-capacity` entries (LRU) |
| the scheduler queue | `buffer-size`, then 429s |

<Note title="Memory is the only limit" type="warning">

  Nothing evicts facts. **Memory is the only limit**, and `nodes`, `order` and `size` in `/api/v1/stats` are the leading indicator — see [Health and monitoring](/docs/operations/runbook/health-and-monitoring).

</Note>

## Levers that reduce footprint

| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| `precision = "float32"` | halves the bytes of every vector and score |
| a smaller embedding dimension | linear in the per-vector cost, and in every tree entry |
| fewer `number-trees` | fewer forest entries per vector, at some recall |
| smaller facts | shorter lock hold times as well as less heap |

## And the one that does not

Raising `buffer-size` does not add throughput — it only moves where requests wait, at the cost of latency and queue memory. See [Load and backpressure](/docs/operations/runbook/backpressure).
